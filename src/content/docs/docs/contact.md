A shared support address and virtual office hours open with the production
service in Q3 2027. Until then, GP-STOR is reachable through the people running
it.

## Talk to your campus lead

Each partner campus has a principal investigator on the project. If your
institution is one of the three, start there.

## Elsewhere in the region

If your campus is not one of the three storage sites, reach out through your
[Great Plains Network](https://www.greatplains.net/) contacts, or through your
own institution's research computing or CI facilitation group. GP-STOR is built
to serve the wider region, and the project wants to hear about workloads it has
not planned for.

## What is useful to bring

Early conversations are more productive with a rough sense of:

- the size of the collection you expect to store, and how fast it grows
- whether the work streams data during computation or archives it afterward
- which of the [access methods](/docs/access) fits your existing workflow
- when you need it, against the [project roadmap](/#roadmap)

## Beta onboarding

Beta users come on in Q2 2027, ahead of open access in Q3. Groups that have
already talked to their campus lead are the ones that get contacted first, so
raising your hand now is worth doing even though the service is not yet running.
