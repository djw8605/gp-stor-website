## Prerequisites

Before getting started with GP-STOR, you'll need:

- [ ] An institutional or research affiliation
- [ ] A clear use case for storage needs
- [ ] Basic familiarity with command-line tools (for some access methods)

## Getting Help

Until the production service opens, the fastest route is a person rather than a
ticket queue:

- Contact the [principal investigator at your campus](/people)
- Reach out through your Great Plains Network contacts
- Check the [FAQ](/docs/faq) for common questions

Virtual office hours and a monitored support address will be announced as part of
the [Q3 2027 open-access milestone](/#roadmap).
